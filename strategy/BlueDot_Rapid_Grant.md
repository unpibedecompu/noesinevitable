**Tell us more about your project.** *(required)*
**Not Ready**
It is a website for the citizen on every spanish-speaking countries for easly contacting its national level representatives by mail for expressing their concern about AI rapid development risks and asking for:

1 - the development of technical capacity to understand and evaluate AI systems
2 - The country support of international agreements regarding the verification and supervisation of AI development equivalent to the nuclear agreements.
3 - Treat this issue as a national security priority, and not merely as a matter of economics or innovation.

**What have you already done?** *(required)*
_Describe concrete progress: code written, pilots run, drafts completed, data collected. If you haven't started, explain what you've done to validate the idea._
**not ready**

The website is up and running at noesinvetable.org (translate to "is not inevitable").
I produced and published one instagram reel and one story proposing it as an easy action for citizens to mitigate AI risks. Currently my instagram account has 1100 followers.
Right now it only covers Argentina, Panama and Uruguay. This is because the contact information of these nations national representatives is easy to scrap. Currently the emails have to be sent separetly to each representative by a button that opens an email provider page or app, this is because it is a free way to implement it. I also set up a free tracking system.
During the first two days after the publications, the reel received around 1500 views. The page had 71 visitors, from which 41 are from spanish-speaking countries and 31 are from countries currently covered by the page. Then 10 people got to open their email provider at least one time. In total 57 emails were sent, 56 to Argentine representatives and one to Uruguay representatives.
